# Actief — pullback-scalp-v1

Owner-approved paper-only redesign, 2026-10-13. Hypotheses, not optimised returns.

## Rules

- Initial account setting 1,000 USDC. This deployment does NOT top up cash,
  erase history, clear losses/halts, or force-close existing positions.
- Broad Jupiter universe: up to 80 sticky tokens, including explicitly seeded SOL.
  Three category lists discover up to 300 rows before deduplication. Up to 10
  eligible tokens receive intensive candle checks; existing holdings get priority.
- Token liquidity >=500k USD, five-minute volume >=50k USD, age >=24h,
  at least 20 buys and 50% buy share. Existing mint/audit checks still apply.
- Actual GeckoTerminal one-minute OHLCV from a pool with >=500k USD liquidity.
  Verify pool mint membership and explicitly request the desired token in USD.
  Fetch 40 candles; evaluate the last 20 closed, consecutive, traded minutes.
  Latest candle close must be at most 90 seconds old. Never use forming candles,
  fabricate missing bars, or use the candle high/low as an executable fill.
- Trend: close above its value 15 minutes earlier; EMA5 above EMA15; EMA15
  above its value three bars earlier.
- Peak among bars 12–17 (0-based in 20-bar window); 1–4 pullback candles before
  the last recovery candle. Impulse >=1.5%; retracement 0.8–5% from that peak.
  Recovery closes above prior candle high and its own open, on >=80% prior volume.
  Current Jupiter price >=recovery trigger and <=0.5% above recovery close.
  Reject price/candle divergence >1.5%. Confirm on a later distinct Jupiter update.
- Stop beneath pullback low, buffered by max(0.1% price, 15% mean 14-bar true range).
  Estimated net stop loss must be 1.5–2.5% of cost and <=5 USD. Price-proportional
  stop conversion is an estimate; actual exit decisions use fresh net sale quotes.
- <=200 USDC per position, four positions, 100 USDC reserve. Fixed quote haircut
  remains 25 bps each side, plus existing fixed network/account setup assumptions.
  Reject total roundtrip drag >0.8%; require room to the prior peak for 3% net gain.
  Neither observed range nor resistance predicts future returns.
- Full exit at +3% net, structural stop, recovery failure or trend break.
  Pattern failure: two subsequent closed bars below reclaim, falling and below
  EMA5. Trend failure: two subsequent closes below EMA15 and falling.
  No mandatory time exit; no aged tightening or partial profit-taking on new trades.
- One trade per peak/trough identity. Five-minute cooldown after loss; thirty
  minutes after two consecutive losses in that token. Winners need a new pattern.
- 60 USD daily loss latch; prior total loss latch retained (350 USD setting).
  100 daily entries is a ceiling, never a quota.

## Resources and limitations

Control interval 15s is a target, NOT a freshness/execution guarantee. Jupiter
pacing remains <=30 requests/minute, shared with Momentum. Cached token requests
can repeat the same observation and do not confirm a signal. Quotes are never cached.
One pending entry quote pair is evaluated per cycle; exit quotes run first inside
the engine. Market collection precedes the engine and can add latency.

GeckoTerminal has one-minute public caching. Use <=8 requests/rolling minute,
<=2 per refresh, 60s normal per-token refresh, 60s backoff on 429. Token pool lookup
also consumes a slot. No paid data subscription or new Render resource is created.
Network failures or stale bars block new entries but not quote-based protective exits.
This is minute-scale scalping, not subsecond high-frequency execution.

FOMO's fee schedule and automated execution connection are NOT implemented or
verified by this paper simulator. Jupiter quotes are hypothetical fills; gaps,
latency and quote-vs-fill differences can exceed stops. No wallet or signing code.

## Transition and validation

Old positions retain their stored adaptive-exits-v2 config until normal closure.
New trades are tagged pullback-scalp-v1. Persistent strategy-only counters include
closed trades, wins, net realised P&L and gross wins/losses. Original ledger and
funding accounting remain intact; no old loss is presented as a new strategy loss.
Config migration is transactional and idempotent; old pending signals/history are
cleared. New setup identities and loss cooldowns survive restart.

Planned forward evaluation: >=14 days AND >=200 closed new-strategy trades before
judging expectancy, costs, drawdown, frequency and token concentration. These are
minimum research sample targets, not proof or an automatically scheduled review.
Do not loosen cost/data filters merely to manufacture frequent trades.
