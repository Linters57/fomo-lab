## Authorized change: adaptive-exits-v2

User asked to inspect losses and replace mandatory time selling with tighter
risk control that leaves upside. Preserve initial 4% stop, 8% take-profit,
position/risk sizing and all account loss limits. At 15 minutes:
- No mandatory time sale.
- Tighten to at least 98% of total entry cost (net exit proceeds).
- Trail 2% below the best net exit quote observed at/after 15 minutes.
- Keep the existing lifetime +3% activation / 1.5% profit trailing stop if tighter.
- Persist the effective stop floor and peaks. Never lower the floor after a dip
  or process restart. If already below the new floor, sell on that priced cycle.
- Quote failures never fabricate an exit. Stops are sampled and cannot guarantee
  a 2% maximum realized loss across gaps, slippage or unavailable routes.

Add a relative cost filter: actual three-minute observed momentum must cover
1.5 times the immediate modeled roundtrip drag. It is an engineering hypothesis,
not an estimate of future return. It may reduce trade count. No paid data/AI added.

custom trailing stops and upward-only stop updates:
https://docs.freqtrade.io/en/latest/strategy-callbacks/#time-based-trailing-stop
