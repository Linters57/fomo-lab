# Actief — active-scalp-v2

Owner-authorized paper experiment, 2026-10-15. These are hypotheses, not optimised
settings or a promise of profitable or frequent trades. No wallet or real orders.

## Changes

- Token liquidity >=200,000 USD; five-minute volume >=10,000 USD. Keep age >=24h,
  at least 20 buys, >=50% buy share, provider reputation/audit and RPC mint checks.
- Keep 20 closed, consecutive, traded one-minute candles, latest close <=90s old,
  no forming/synthetic candles, and <=1.5% quote/candle divergence.
- Trend: close above the close 15 minutes ago and EMA5 > EMA15.
- Recovery: peak in bars 10–17 of the 20-bar window, 1–6 pullback bars,
  retracement 0.3–5%, last bar bullish and above the prior close, volume >=80%
  of prior bar. A current provider price must also stay above the prior close.
  No minimum 1.5% prior impulse or 3% room below the prior peak is required.
- Breakout: same trend/bullish/volume tests; last closed bar and current provider
  price above the highest high in the prior ten bars. Local low in the last
  three bars defines structural risk.
- Both: provider price within 0.5% of last close, new distinct provider update on
  a later cycle, and <=0.5% chase from signal. One entry per peak/trough or
  breakout-pivot identity. Recovery and breakout get separate trade labels/results.
- Stop below the local low by max(0.1% price, 15% mean 14-bar true range).
  No minimum 1.5% stop distance. Reject if modeled net risk exceeds 2% of cost
  or 2 USD. Maximum inlay 100 USD; four positions; 100 USD cash reserve.
- Full exit at +2% NET, structural stop, pattern failure or trend failure.
  No forced time exit. The fixed 25-bps haircut per quote, network and initial
  account cost assumptions remain. Total modeled return-trip cost <=0.8%.
  First-use account rent can make a new token too expensive at 100 USD even with
  zero quoted fees; do not hide rent, seed an account, or loosen cost checks to
  manufacture trades. Existing previously paid-for modeled accounts are reused.
- Existing daily/total loss latches, 100-entry daily ceiling and loss cooldowns
  remain. One entry quote pair per cycle; rotate attempted tokens fairly so a
  repeat quote rejection cannot starve other signals.

## Data fixes and diagnostics

Use pools containing the exact mint with >=200,000 USD liquidity. Rank qualifying
pools by recent volume, then liquidity. Conflicting, stale, gapped or inactive
candles remain invalid; mark that pool unusable for five minutes and try a known
alternative on the next paced request. Never merge conflicting candles or turn
old candles into fresh observations.

Up to six intensive tokens, prioritising holdings, within the existing eight
GeckoTerminal requests per rolling minute and two per refresh. Pool discovery
counts against the same budget. Keep 429 backoff and exact token/USD orientation.

Durable diagnostics record candidate evaluations by outcome, separately from
rejected entry rechecks. Show total since revision and local-day counts. A token
can be counted in many cycles; these are not unique tokens or missed trades.
Signals, limit/held/cooldown outcomes and data failures remain distinguishable.
Preselection counts and provider failures remain in discovery diagnostics.

Migration preserves all cash, equity, history, controls, losses and halt flags.
Archive prior strategy results; reset only new-strategy counters/pending patterns.
Open v1 or earlier positions retain their stored config and original exit code in
scalping_v1.py. They never count as v2 results. Migration is transactional and
idempotent, including across restarts and later dashboard overrides.

Research target: 10–30 entries/day, never a quota. Check frequency/data coverage
after 72 hours; 50–100 closes are an initial diagnosis only. Longer forward
evaluation remains necessary before claiming an improvement. These are evaluation
targets, not an automatically scheduled task. Jupiter quote marks are hypothetical
fills; execution latency, gaps and fill costs can exceed the modeled stop.
