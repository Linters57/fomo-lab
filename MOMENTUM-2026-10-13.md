# Momentum runner v2 — owner-approved paper experiment

Revision: momentum-runner-v2. Approved 2026-10-13 after reviewing 39 trades.
This is a forward test, not an optimised strategy or proven improvement.

## Entries

Keep the original 5-minute momentum range (1–6%), liquidity, volume, safety,
position/risk limits and 60-second control interval. Require a distinct later
provider update for confirmation. Also require 15 minutes of provider-time
history, >=12 observations, no gap >90 seconds, same source pool throughout,
positive 15-minute change, two successive price increases, price above EMA10,
and rising EMA10 compared with three observations earlier. These EMAs use
sampled prices, not OHLCV candles or VWAP. No extra paid market-data feed.

Keep up to eight selected tokens in 30-minute watch slots, with batch updates
for watchlist and holdings. The 15-minute warmup prevents immediate entries
on restart/discovery. Unsafe current updates never inherit old approval.

## Exits

- Initial stop retains configured value (default 12% of entry cost).
- At configured net profit target (default 24%), quote selling exactly 75%
  of the token quantity and separately quote valuing the exact remainder.
  Only commit if both quotes are fresh and the partial sale reaches its
  proportional net target. An unavailable leg means no partial fill.
- Allocate entry cost by exact sold-quantity ratio. The remaining cost and
  quantity stay on the ledger, including integer rounding. Book partial P&L
  immediately; closed-trade/win counters update only on final closure and
  include BOTH legs' P&L. SELL events distinguish partial versus final and
  carry a common trade_id; CSV includes those fields.
- The remaining 25% uses a ratcheting stop at 8% below highest fresh net
  remainder quote, with the original proportional stop as a lower bound.
  It closes no later than two additional hours after partial profit-taking.
  A quote outage never closes/deletes a holding or invents a price.
- At 60 minutes, a full position may extend to 120 minutes from opening only
  if its quoted net value exceeds entry cost AND current fresh trend passes
  the same 15-minute confirmation. Otherwise retain the normal time exit.
  Extended holdings use an 8% ratcheting quote stop. Reaching the profit
  target can still create the 25% runner. Stop decisions take priority if
  an existing protective floor is already breached.
- Exit quotes and protective decisions remain active during loss halts or
  a manual pause. Entries on fresh data stay blocked by existing limits.

8% is an experimental middle value between the analysed 6% and 10% cases.
The runner's historical advantage was dominated by one exceptional token move.
Longer holdings can prevent subsequent buys; no isolated historical gains are
assumed to be additive portfolio gains. Stops are decision thresholds, not
execution guarantees. Pool/candle data in the review differed from execution
quotes; the implementation uses actual quote-only paper marks for exact amounts.

## Transition and observability

Idempotent activation preserves cash, funding, history, losses, halt flags and
all original closed-trade counts. Existing holdings retain the legacy exit
contract until they close naturally. Clear only old pending signals and sampled
history; record a version baseline and separate new-version realised P&L,
partial sales, completed positions and win count. Do not top up or reset capital.

Dashboard shows new-version performance, partial sells, remaining cost, current
trailing floor, hold extensions and full-trade P&L when a runner finally closes.
Actief's independent scalping rules and ledger remain unchanged.
